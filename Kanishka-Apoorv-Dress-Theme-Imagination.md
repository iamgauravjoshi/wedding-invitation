# ✨ Kanishka ❤️ Apoorv — Five Chapters, One Story ✨

*Dear family and friends,*

A wedding isn't a single day. It's a story told over five evenings — and every one of you is a chapter in it. So here's the plot, and here's what the cast wears.

---

## 🩷 Chapter One — LAGAN
*The first promise, written in cloth.*

Imagine the courtyard the moment the sun begins to melt: marigold garlands swinging, brass thalis gleaming, and everyone wearing the colour the sun paints on a wall just before dusk.

**She** — *Bandhej (Chundari print)*: little tied knots across the fabric, like promises someone tied by hand and never untied.
**He** — *Kurta Pajama*: easy, light, and full of grace.

> *This is the page where our families first hold hands.*

---

## 💛 Chapter Two — HALDI
*Yellow morning, pink hearts.*

A whole day dressed in **HOT PINK**. Think gulab, think fuchsia, think the kind of colour that photographs like a firework. Shears, marigolds, laughter, and everyone glowing in the same outrageous shade.

**She** — Hot pink, any outfit.
**He** — Hot pink, any outfit. Yes, you too. *Especially* you. 😄

> *Rule number one: if you can't find it in hot pink, dye it.*

---

## 💃 Chapter Three — SANGEET
*One night, and the floor is ours.*

The lights drop, the dholak starts, and suddenly the room is all twirls and tassels.

**She** — *Lehanga*: the skirt that does the dancing before you do.
**He** — Anything you like — though a **three-piece kurta pajama** is warmly recommended. When the ladies arrive in lehengas, you'll want a silhouette worth standing beside.

> *This is the chapter with the best music.*

---

## ❤️ Chapter Four — PHERE
*The quiet centre of it all.*

No costume needed here — only you.

**She** — Anything your heart chooses.
**He** — Anything your heart chooses.

Come in whatever feels true. Come with a full heart. Around the fire, that's the only outfit anyone will remember.

---

## ✨ Chapter Five — RECEPTION
*And then, the encore.*

The room turns into a ballroom — chandeliers, candles, and a hundred people who all dressed up just to celebrate us.

**She** — *Saree*: six yards of pure elegance, draped in whatever colour you love.
**He** — *Formal three-piece suit*: waistcoat, jacket, and a smile that says you meant to look this sharp all along.

---

## 🎀 So, to sum it up…

| Function | 👩 She | 👨 He |
|---|---|---|
| 🩷 Lagan | Bandhej (Chundari print) | Kurta Pajama |
| 💛 Haldi | Anything hot pink | Anything hot pink |
| 💃 Sangeet | Lehanga | Any — three-piece kurta recommended |
| ❤️ Phere | Anything you love | Anything you love |
| ✨ Reception | Saree | Formal three-piece suit |

**The theme isn't a rule — it's an invitation to be part of the picture.** 💛

We can't wait to see you. Come dressed, come dancing, come hungry for the story.

*With love,*
**Kanishka & Apoorv** ❤️🎉
