Hello everyone! ❤️

A wedding isn't one day — it's a story told over five evenings, and every one of you is a chapter in it. Here's the plot… and here's what the cast wears. 😊

🩷 *CHAPTER 1 — LAGAN*
_The first promise, written in cloth._

Imagine the courtyard at golden hour — marigold garlands swinging, brass thalis gleaming, and everyone wearing the colour the sun paints on a wall just before dusk.

👩 *Bandhej (Chundari print)* — little tied knots across the fabric, like promises tied by hand and never untied.
👨 *Kurta Pajama* — easy, light, full of grace.

💛 *CHAPTER 2 — HALDI*
_Yellow morning, pink hearts._

A whole day dressed in *HOT PINK*. Think gulab, think fuchsia, think a colour that photographs like a firework. Shears, marigolds, laughter — and everyone glowing in the same outrageous shade.

👩 Anything hot pink
👨 Anything hot pink. Yes, you too. *Especially* you. 😄

💃 *CHAPTER 3 — SANGEET*
_One night, and the floor is ours._

The lights drop, the dholak starts, and suddenly the room is all twirls and tassels.

👩 *Lehanga* — the skirt that starts dancing before you do.
👨 Anything you like — though a *three-piece kurta pajama* is warmly recommended. When the ladies arrive in lehengas, you'll want a silhouette worth standing beside. 🤵

❤️ *CHAPTER 4 — PHERE*
_The quiet centre of it all._

No costume needed here — only you.

👩 Anything your heart chooses
👨 Anything your heart chooses

Come in whatever feels true, with a full heart. Around the fire, that's the only outfit anyone will remember.

✨ *CHAPTER 5 — RECEPTION*
_And then, the encore._

The room turns into a ballroom — chandeliers, candles, and a hundred people who all dressed up just to celebrate us.

👩 *Saree* — six yards of pure elegance, draped in whatever colour you love.
👨 *Formal three-piece suit* — waistcoat, jacket, and a smile that says you meant to look this sharp all along.
